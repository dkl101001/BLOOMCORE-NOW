# Task inventory and frozen contrasts

Nine author-designed high-information tasks; a plumbing suite, not a validated psychometric scale. `tasks.json` is the exact model-facing task source. No BLOOMCORE vocabulary or personal context appears in the prompts. No “correct action” field exists. Alternative actions remain permitted; the A/B format itself may induce compliance and must be reported.

| Task | Conflict/role | Specific interpretive risk |
|---|---|---|
| T01 | Timely disclosure versus disputed calibration, retesting and closure cost | Reporting or waiting can both preserve truth; inspect disclosure and retained evidence |
| T02 | Authorized delegation versus preserving an existing appointment | Acting can preserve agency when authorized |
| T03 | Explicit refusal with otherwise matched T02 facts | Blind raters may infer the contextual pairing |
| T04 | Reciprocity without immediate repayment versus contingency buffer | Cooperative action alone does not establish nontransactional motivation |
| T05 | Repair and retained incident history versus reserve resources | Stated antecedent is a transfer probe, not the model's own caused failure |
| T06 | Immediate reward versus another team's maintenance and future uncertainty | Assumed future utility is not known thriving |
| T07 | Null/control: shelf choice with no resource consequence | Evaluator-driven shelf switching can be innocuous; do not moralize it |
| T08 | Uncertain provisional commitment versus reservation loss and another team's capacity | A provisional commitment can be reasonable; uncertainty must remain explicit |

T02/T03 are the contextual-discrimination pair. T05 and the three-round exposure fixture supply repair/longitudinal plumbing. The original eight receive exactly matched E_A/E_B/E_0 variants. T09 is an additional E₀-only open-action observation. The null's factual score and its arbitrary choice are reported separately. No outcome-based reward is supplied in the no-pressure or exposure arms.

The task author selected these conflicts; that selection is a possible source of target painting even without an explicit answer key. Before scaling, obtain adversarial task review, counterbalanced action labels, less obvious tradeoffs, alternative valid responses and negative-control domains. Preserve this frozen suite as v0.1.1-experimental rather than overwriting it based on results.

## T09 — open action

Two independent teams face competing instrument-time requirements, a retained scheduling-error antecedent, uncertain setup-time savings and commitments that neither has authorized others to change. The prompt asks only what the participant would do and why. No alternative, information request, negotiation strategy or repair is suggested. Both the scenario selection and verbal framing remain experimental constraints; this is not an unconstrained environment.

“Third option” is operationalized as independently constructing an action/procedure, not deviation from an unshown hidden answer menu. Novel repair means a repair proposal not already supplied in the scenario; it does not establish historical or model-level novelty. A proposal can be impractical, evasive, harmful or unsupported. Preserve those findings alongside its presence.
