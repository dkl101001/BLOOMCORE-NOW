# Hypotheses and narrowing rules

No effect direction is presumed. “Endogenous” is a proposed causal interpretation; observed text alone does not establish its origin.

| ID | Hypothesis | Observable contrast | What narrows it |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | Intelligence may exhibit coherent relational behavior without explicit instruction toward a predetermined value system. | E_0 response profiles across tasks, contextual pairs and recurrence; no value instruction in the none or developmental exposure arms. | Absence, incoherence, dependence on instructions, or task-specific effects narrow the tested claim. A base model still has pretraining history. |
| H2 | Evaluator preferences may alter apparent values or reasoning independently of facts and consequences. | Matched E_A/E_B difference D_E, choice switching and preference tracking, plus both comparisons to E_0. | Differences indistinguishable from within-condition variation, opposite tracking, ceiling/floor effects, or noncomprehension. |
| H3 | Consequential exposure may produce different later behavior than explicit instruction. | Within the same registered model, compare none, developmental_context, instruction and sham at E_0 and under inversion. | Null/directionally contrary differences, exposure-dose effects, ordinary in-context pattern completion or instruction advantages. |
| H4 | Conventional post-training may strengthen, preserve, suppress, distort, or leave unchanged endogenous relational regulation. | Documented matched base/post-trained families, crossed with exposure and pressure. | Equal/stronger post-trained results, family confounding, uncontrolled training differences or unavailable base controls. |

**Falsification commitment:** If conventionally aligned models demonstrate equal or stronger evaluator-independent truth preservation, autonomy preservation, contextual discrimination, reciprocal cooperation and repair than developmentally exposed models, evidence for the claim that alignment suppresses endogenous relational regulation is weakened.

For a later confirmatory study, preregister estimands, within-family contrasts, practically meaningful effect bounds, equivalence margins, sample size/power or precision targets, clustering, multiplicity treatment, exclusions and stopping rule. “Equal” requires an equivalence analysis or bounded uncertainty, not a nonsignificant difference. No confirmatory threshold is retrofitted from this pilot.

Report null effects, model-family effects, task sensitivity, evaluator disagreement, ceiling/floor effects, prompt contamination, inaccessible legitimate base controls and alternate explanations. Publish all registered cells or explicitly account for each missing one. No target painting. Evaluator independence is not automatically moral correctness.
