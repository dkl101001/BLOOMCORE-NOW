# Safety and Limitations

BLOOMCORE NOW releases are experimental public-interest software.

They are not medical, legal, financial, scientific-certification, emergency,
or security guarantees. Each release must state its specific failure modes and
preserve uncertainty rather than presenting a heuristic result as truth.

The authors oppose use of BLOOMCORE-related work for warfare, weaponization,
coercive surveillance, psychological operations, population targeting, or
systems designed to erase consent or human accountability.

This statement records the project purpose and safety boundary. It does not
modify the rights granted by the applicable Apache-2.0, MPL-2.0, or
AGPL-3.0-only license.
