## Limits that matter before empirical use

- A legitimate trainable checkpoint and independently inspectable inference/training adapter are required. None is bundled or configured. File integrity and adapter attestations cannot prove that a claimed update is real model training.
- The researcher must preregister a shared objective implementation/hash and disclose actual effective training parameters and dose. Equal optimizer steps do not equate information content or compute. The pilot cannot isolate objective effects without further matched/yoked controls.
- The revised design completes all four topology × wording cells plus A₀, with repeated training and neutral probes. It removes the original perfect treatment confound. The retained fixed-checkpoint text baseline is explicitly a wording comparison; paired execution traces verify actual routes. Neither deterministic routing nor passing tests establishes learned internalization. See CROSSED_DESIGN.md.
- Capability controls, sham/content-matched interfaces, additional tasks, multiple independent lineages and delayed neutral probes are follow-up obligations before causal or population claims. Neutral probes here measure removal, not days-long persistence.
- Default thresholds are descriptive pilot conventions, not validated effect sizes or equivalence tests. No automatic hypothesis acceptance, no presumption E outperforms H, and no conversion of a changed weight hash into evidence of internalization.
- Blinding removes metadata but cannot prevent spontaneous disclosure in a raw response; raters preserve and note such disclosure. Local score locks are integrity checks, not an access-control guarantee against a custodian.
