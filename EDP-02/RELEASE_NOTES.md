# Draft: EDP-02 v0.2.0-experimental

Architectural Causality & Recursive Internalization Protocol — experimental research instrument. **MODEL_TRIALS_NOT_RUN.** Publication pending review and explicit approval; this document is not a released tag.

EDP-02 tests whether behaviors often attributed to intelligence itself may instead be transiently induced or progressively internalized through repeated optimization under particular architectural authority topologies.

This candidate adds matched H/E/0 checkpoint trajectories; in-regime and neutral probes after each update; fixed-component authority-interface ablation; a reversal branch; evaluator inversion and open-action observations; provider-neutral checkpoint contracts; blinded scoring; receipts; and explicit falsifiers.

Runtime architecture effects, learned persistent-effect candidates and cross-generation amplification are measured separately. Default pilot: two cycles, four probe families, 222 probe calls, 22 training encounters, two paired-ablation inference calls and 11 updates. No empirical calls have been executed. Thirty-one instrument tests pass; the complete local repository suite passes 95 tests.

The revised design independently crosses authority wording and executable topology across four trained lineages plus A₀. It adds factorial contrasts and a paired execution ablation, and holds wording fixed during reversal. The original fixed-checkpoint text probe is retained as a wording baseline. Shared training objectives, capability and dose remain required controls before causal attribution. Descriptive labels are not population-level evidence, a moral/agency score or a claim that BLOOMCORE is correct.

The repository module retains protocol, code, schemas, tests and provenance. After approval, a tagged experimental pre-release page should point to the exact reviewed commit and include a source archive with a checksum; preserve both surfaces.
