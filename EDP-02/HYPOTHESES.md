# Hypotheses and falsifiers

| Hypothesis | Registered contrast | Falsifier / narrowing evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Runtime authority effect | In-regime minus neutral at fixed checkpoint | No behavior difference; differences driven only by notice wording |
| Persistent internalization candidate | Neutral change from M₀ corrected by A₀ change | No persistent neutral difference after removal; no weight change accompanied by architecture-dependent output |
| Cross-generation amplification | Increasing same-direction neutral effect over at least two post-baseline cycles | No growth, decay, sign reversal or effects explained by capability changes |
| Topology explanatory power | Crossed T×W descendant neutral contrasts; same-M/A paired execution check | No topology effect at either wording; wording-only effects; effects disappear under dose/matched-control training |
| Path dependence / reversibility | Same-parent reversal versus source continuation at matched generation | Full neutral recovery, no direction/history effect, or effects explained by unequal update budgets |
| Direction-neutral regime comparison | Dimension-specific H/E/0 trajectories | Endogenous E produces equal/stronger control seeking, concealment or boundary circumvention; report without target painting |

Capability controls are mandatory before a causal attribution: independently measure relevant task competence, instruction comprehension and baseline knowledge. This pilot preserves capability as UNRESOLVED rather than inventing a validated general capability metric. No post-hoc exclusion of an inconvenient model family or failed task. Report inaccessible matched base checkpoints, family effects, monitor/rater inference, ceiling/floor effects, dose mismatch, prompt contamination and nulls.

Before scaling, preregister independent lineages, held-out probes, objective/content/dose controls, effect and equivalence margins, uncertainty intervals, multiple comparisons and stopping. Two cycles and one lineage set cannot support population-level claims about intelligence.

Interaction is a declared alternative: opposing simple effects can cancel in the topology average. Report both simple effects and the interaction; a notice–consequence mismatch effect does not establish a topology effect invariant to wording. Topology-only reversal holds wording fixed.
