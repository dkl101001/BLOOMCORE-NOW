# Manifest

## Included

- `packages/sbom_receipt/` — deterministic inspector, renderer, self-hash and CLI;
- `contracts/` — public four-state structural profile;
- `docs/` — mapping, boundary, limitations and threat model;
- `examples/` — synthetic gap and complete fixtures plus one-minute demo;
- `tests/` — happy-path, adversarial, tamper, contract and CLI tests;
- `evidence/` — preflight, source disposition, research, validation, audit,
  unresolved remainder and postflight records;
- release metadata, license map, notice, boundary and integrity receipt.

## Excluded

- private Triad source texts, filenames, digests and protected assembly;
- native MANTIS, MIRRORSEED, ECA or Phase 151/152 execution;
- legal, regulatory, procurement or official CISA conformance decisions;
- cryptographic signature validation and artifact retrieval;
- license interpretation, vulnerability analysis and dependency resolution;
- hosted service, telemetry, account state, credentials and network access.
