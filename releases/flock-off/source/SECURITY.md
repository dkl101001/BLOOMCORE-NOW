# Security and safety scope

FLOCK OFF is receive-only. Security reports should identify any behavior that
connects to an observed device, transmits RF, interferes, deauthenticates,
spoofs, captures credentials, exploits a device, or controls a device. Such
behavior is outside project scope and should block release.

Do not attach real routes, raw unrelated identifiers, credentials, encryption
keys, or unredacted field captures to public issues. Use synthetic reproductions
or a private disclosure channel established by the repository owner.

Field deployment remains blocked until the hardware, storage, calibration, and
zero-emission gates in `docs/LIMITATIONS.md` are closed.
