# FLOCK OFF — The Transparency Desk

**We, too, would like to live a private life.**

Version 0.1 · 2026-10-27 · Prepared for Frazer Love

An independent public-accountability toolkit. This is a draft companion to FLOCK OFF, not a change to its software or release roadmap. No requests have been sent and no deployment-specific allegations are made here.

The aim: make procurement, oversight, retention, sharing, cost, and renewal decisions understandable and answerable. Every request should resolve a concrete public-interest question.

## Start here

1. Select one agency, one deployment or contract, and a bounded date range.
2. Check its existing public documents. Save the source URL, retrieval date, document date, and relevant page or section.
3. Use the assessment below to identify the most consequential unanswered questions.
4. Send one focused request through the agency's verified official channel after reviewing recipients, scope, contact details, and any fee terms.
5. Log the response, preserve originals privately, and publish only reviewed, redacted evidence.

No automatic submissions, duplicate request storms, invented residents, or involuntary sharing. A coordinated question can have many genuine supporters without generating many identical requests.

## Transparency Impact Assessment

**Independent questionnaire — voluntary, not a government form or binding requirement.**

Agency: [name]  
Vendor / product: [name or unknown]  
Deployment / contract reference: [ID or description]  
Review period: [start–end]  
Prepared by: [name / organization]  
Last reviewed: [date]

Please attach or link the supporting document for each answer. Distinguish current practice from contractual permission and planned practice. “Unknown,” “not held,” and “not applicable” are useful answers when explained.

| ID | Question | Evidence requested |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T01 | Who authorized this deployment, when, and through what decision process? | Approval, meeting record, resolution, or delegated authority |
| T02 | What public purpose and measurable outcome were specified? | Adopted policy, project proposal, evaluation criteria |
| T03 | What equipment and services were purchased, leased, trialed, donated, or grant-funded? | Contract, order, inventory summary, funding record |
| T04 | What is the total committed cost, including installation, subscriptions, maintenance, and exit costs? | Executed price schedules, amendments, invoices |
| T05 | What data is collected, inferred, retained, and deleted? | Current data inventory and retention/deletion policy |
| T06 | Who may access data or search results, for what purposes, and under whose approval? | Access policy, role descriptions, audit procedure |
| T07 | Which outside entities may receive data or access, including through vendor-mediated sharing? | Sharing agreements and current configuration documentation, with sensitive details redacted |
| T08 | How are misuse, incorrect matches, and complaints recorded and reviewed? | Complaint procedure, aggregate audit or evaluation reports |
| T09 | What evidence supports claimed benefits, and what limitations or unsuccessful outcomes were recorded? | Existing evaluations, methodology, aggregate findings |
| T10 | Who owns the records, and what happens to them at termination? | Ownership, deletion, portability, and termination provisions |
| T11 | When does the contract renew, what is the notice window, and who can approve renewal? | Renewal clause and responsible office |
| T12 | How can residents participate in review and obtain corrections to public information? | Published participation and correction process |

Assessment states: **documented / partially documented / unanswered / disputed / not applicable**. These describe evidence availability, not a legal finding, compliance certification, or verdict on the system.

## Focused records-request draft

Replace every bracketed field before use. This template does not assert jurisdiction-specific deadlines, exemptions, fee entitlements, or statutory requirements. Local rules must be checked before adding such claims.

To: [verified agency records office]

Subject: Records concerning [deployment or contract], [date range]

Hello,

I request existing records held by [agency] concerning [specific deployment or contract] during [start date] through [end date]:

1. The executed agreement, incorporated schedules, amendments, and purchase orders.
2. The approval or authorization record and any associated public meeting materials.
3. The current policy governing retention, authorized use, external sharing, and review of access.
4. Existing evaluation reports addressing the deployment's stated outcomes or limitations.
5. Records identifying the renewal date, notice window, and responsible decision-making office.

Electronic copies or links to the exact responsive records would be welcome. I am seeking existing records, rather than asking your office to create a new analysis. Please omit any category already satisfied by the following exact documents: [links / identifiers, or delete this sentence].

Please provide an estimate before incurring charges above [amount]. If clarification would materially reduce the search burden, please contact me at [designated contact]. If portions are withheld, please identify the stated basis and provide any portions you can release.

Thank you,

[name]

[designated contact]

## Council question draft

Before [contract / deployment] is approved or renewed, will the agency publish the executed terms, total cost, retention and sharing policies, and existing outcome evaluations? Please identify who owns that review and where residents can find the next decision date.

## One follow-up draft

Hello,

I am following up on [reference], submitted [date], concerning [scope]. Could you confirm its status, any clarification needed, and the expected next step? Documents already supplied are logged here: [private or reviewed public reference].

Thank you,

[name]

Set a chosen reminder date separately from any verified legal deadline. Review agency updates before following up. Do not automatically resend.

## Evidence and contradiction ledger

For each conclusion, record:

- **Claim ID and exact statement.** Keep its scope narrow.
- **Basis:** document / direct observation / attributed statement / inference / unresolved question.
- **Source custody:** original URL or file, issuing body, document and retrieval dates, page or section, SHA-256 for saved public documents, and any later replacement.
- **Lineage:** original source and derivative copies. Five mirrors of one record remain one underlying source.
- **Support and contradiction:** preserve both, including agency/vendor explanations and superseded versions.
- **Disposition:** supported within stated scope / partially supported / disputed / unverified / corrected / withdrawn.
- **Review:** reviewer, date, reasoning, and correction history.

“We have not received a response” is a status. It does not establish concealment, illegality, or the truth of an allegation. A record describing a permitted capability does not prove that capability was used. A map pin or RF candidate does not prove ownership or surveillance use.

## Publication review

Public summaries should show the question, source-backed answer, unresolved gap, and next public decision. Preserve the original privately and publish a redacted copy with a revision history.

Remove observer routes, private contact details, credentials, unnecessary personal information, plate data, and identifiers of bystanders. Do not publish security-sensitive configuration details merely because they appear in a response. Obtain consent before naming contributors. Keep recipient-facing contact details separate from the public tracker.

Offer a correction channel. Material disputes should appear beside the claim, not disappear into an archive. Never label a deployment unlawful or an individual culpable solely because a request is unanswered.

Residents deserve to understand the systems operating in their communities: who approved them, what they cost, what they collect, who gets access, and when the decision comes up again.

The Transparency Desk organizes those questions and the records that answer them. Sources stay visible. Uncertainty stays visible. Corrections stay visible.
