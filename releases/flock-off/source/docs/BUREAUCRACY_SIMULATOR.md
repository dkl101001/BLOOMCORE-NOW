# Bureaucracy Simulator — optional satire

Choose **Bureaucracy Simulator (satire demo)** in the records desk. This nonmodal demo uses a bounded, seedable shuffle of fictional departments and forms followed by the final JSON stage, never your case, files, or submissions. Its examples are read-only; do not enter personal information.

Continue through each fictional stage and the final JSON tab. Only **Finish demo**, at the final stage, displays **ERROR RT-418: INSUFFICIENT CEREMONIAL INDENTATION**, together with an explanation that the rule is fictional. The real JSON importer accepts valid tab-indented JSON.

**Exit demo — keep progress** and the window close button are available throughout. Reopening the simulator in the same records-desk session retains the current step and final result. There is no demo autosave across quitting the entire records desk; no real case data is stored or changed. Completion does not discard progress or require restarting.

The simulator does not validate, submit, publish, or network anything. It cannot replace real case review or export approval. The collaboration successor remains unpublished pending expanded qualification and independent security review.
