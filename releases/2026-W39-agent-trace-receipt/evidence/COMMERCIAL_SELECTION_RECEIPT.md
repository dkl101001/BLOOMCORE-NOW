# Commercial Selection Receipt

Selected weekly build: **Agent Trace Receipt**  
Commercial disposition: `COMMERCIAL_PRIORITY`  
Frazer-dependency gate: **PASS**

The public MVP is repeatable, passive, and self-serve. A commercial path is
credible because adjacent budgets already exist in observability, AppSec,
agent evaluation, and compliance evidence. This remains a hypothesis until
buyer interviews or paid pilots establish demand.

The design fails the gate if customers require Frazer to interpret each trace,
provide emergency incident response, maintain continuous account presence, or
reconstruct organization-specific policy by hand. Those services are excluded.
