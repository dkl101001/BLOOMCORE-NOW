## One-sentence legibility

1. **PLEDGE RECEIPT:** Turn an AI code, roadmap or public promise into a
   deterministic receipt showing what is testable, partial, merely declarative,
   removed or structurally weakened.
2. **AGENT SCOPE CANARY:** Compare an agent evaluation's declared network/tool
   boundary with a safe preflight capability probe and stop before a
   contradiction becomes a real-world action.
3. **FRONTIER PACE LEDGER:** Bind public pacing promises to dated releases,
   incidents and revisions so unclosed gaps remain visible.

bounded and useful now. SCOPE CANARY has the strongest commercial path but
remains liminal until safe probe and portability boundaries close. PACE LEDGER
is closed because a credible operation presently requires continual expert
curation and would fail the hard Frazer-dependency gate.
