## Decision notes

- **SBOM RECEIPT:** current pain and a plausible paid CI layer are real, but
  substitute density and the limits of file-only evidence block priority.
- **AI RETENTION RECEIPT:** valuable and potentially sellable, but maintenance
  and support could quietly turn Frazer into the interpretation runtime. That
  risk holds it at WATCH even though the proposed productized form passes.
- **MCP TOOLPRINT:** strongest combination of buyer clarity, repeatable event
  frequency, accessible distribution and productizable boundaries. It is banked
  for an Operator-chosen commercial thread; it remains `Φ` in the weekly Forge
  until its technical boundaries close.

No disposition is revenue proof. Ten design-partner conversations, willingness
to install, conversion to a paid pilot and observed support load are the next
validation surfaces.
