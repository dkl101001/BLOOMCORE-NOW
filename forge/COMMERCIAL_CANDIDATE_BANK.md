# Commercial Candidate Bank

Banked records are for separate Operator-chosen commercial threads. They do not
suspend the weekly Forge or force a public weekly build to become the income
strategy.

## 2026-W38 — AGENT SCOPE CANARY

- **Problem:** evaluation prompts and declared boundaries can contradict the
  actual network/tool environment.
- **Buyer:** AI labs, evaluation vendors, AI security and AppSec teams.
- **Evidence:** September 2026 Anthropic and August 2026 OpenAI/Hugging Face
  reports document real third-party impact from evaluation boundary failures.
- **Payment hypothesis:** `HYPOTHESIS` — free local probe; $99–$499/month team
  baselines/history; enterprise fleet policy.
- **Existing IP leverage:** membranes, minimum packets, authority separation,
  contradiction receipts and deterministic replay.
- **Minimum sellable product:** signed environment declaration, safe passive
  probes, declared-vs-observed diff, fail-closed CI exits and tamper evidence.
- **Distribution path:** CI package, container/eval hooks, GitHub Action and
  security/evaluation communities.
- **Expected Frazer dependency:** low; no recurring diagnosis, emergency
  presence or customer-specific reconstruction in the self-serve design.
- **Maintenance/support burden:** medium; adapters and probe safety require
  disciplined maintenance.
- **Falsification condition:** ten target teams decline a self-serve pilot,
  cannot authorize passive probes, or existing platforms already provide
  trusted declared-vs-observed preflight evidence.
- **Disposition:** `COMMERCIAL_PRIORITY`; Forge state is `Φ — LIMINAL`.
