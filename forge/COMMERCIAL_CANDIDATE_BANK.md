# Commercial Candidate Bank

Commercial records are preserved for Operator-directed build threads. Pricing
and revenue paths are hypotheses until validated. Banking neither suspends the
weekly Forge nor turns a weekly candidate into the income strategy.

## 2026-W37 — MCP TOOLPRINT

- **Problem:** MCP tool definitions and effective capabilities can change after
  review, while high-risk permission combinations remain difficult to inspect.
- **Buyer:** AI platform, application-security and developer-infrastructure
  teams deploying MCP-connected agents.
- **Evidence:** CSA's May 4, 2026 research note documents command injection,
  tool poisoning, rug-pull definition changes and weak authorization; current
  practitioner discussion asks for definition diffing, inventory and hashing.
- **Payment hypothesis:** `HYPOTHESIS` — free local scanner; $49–$199/month team
  CI policy and history; $500–$2,000/month enterprise fleet inventory.
- **Existing IP leverage:** deterministic receipts, contradiction preservation,
  boundary inspection, hash-bound replay and additive custody patterns.
- **Minimum sellable product:** local config discovery, normalized toolprint,
  permission-risk rules, signed baseline diff and CI exit codes.
- **Distribution path:** GitHub Action, package registries, MCP/security
  communities and direct AppSec evaluation.
- **Expected Frazer dependency:** low; rules and adapters must be productized.
  No recurring diagnosis or emergency presence is required.
- **Maintenance/support burden:** medium; MCP host formats and attack patterns
  evolve, but adapters can be versioned independently.
- **Falsification condition:** ten target teams decline a self-serve pilot or
  existing scanners already provide trusted definition baselines and diffs at
  no marginal cost.
- **Disposition:** `COMMERCIAL_PRIORITY`; Forge state remains `Φ — LIMINAL`.

## 2026-W37 — BLOOMCORE SBOM RECEIPT

- **Problem:** CycloneDX schema validity does not answer whether the new CISA
  2026 minimum-element fields are present or whether a requirement needs
  evidence outside the file.
- **Buyer:** software suppliers, AppSec teams, procurement engineering and
  compliance-operations teams exchanging SBOMs.
- **Evidence:** CISA published the replacement baseline on July 29, 2026;
  maintainers opened profile-support work within days.
- **Payment hypothesis:** `HYPOTHESIS` — open local CLI; $49–$199/month CI and
  policy packs; enterprise portfolio reporting priced separately.
- **Existing IP leverage:** evidence receipts, explicit unknown-state handling,
  deterministic replay, non-sovereign metrics and custody membranes.
- **Minimum sellable product:** CycloneDX JSON structural profile, policy
  override file, CI annotations, trend history and shareable receipts.
- **Distribution path:** GitHub Action, PyPI, SBOM practitioner communities and
  integrations with generation tools.
- **Expected Frazer dependency:** low; standards interpretation must be encoded
  and reviewed, not delivered as bespoke consulting.
- **Maintenance/support burden:** medium; mappings and standards updates require
  maintenance, while the local engine is low-support.
- **Falsification condition:** target teams report no decision value beyond
  existing schema validators, or cannot identify a budget owner for workflow
  automation.
- **Disposition:** `COMMERCIAL_WATCH`; Forge state is `1 — ACTIVE`.

## 2026-W37 — AI RETENTION RECEIPT

- **Problem:** AI data retention depends on provider, product, endpoint,
  storage flags, approved controls and third-party services; policy pages are
  difficult to turn into a workload decision.
- **Buyer:** privacy, security, procurement and AI-platform teams.
- **Evidence:** current OpenAI and Anthropic documentation each describes
  multiple retention paths, approval-gated controls and exceptions.
- **Payment hypothesis:** `HYPOTHESIS` — $99–$499/month per team for monitored
  provider matrices, evidence snapshots and change alerts.
- **Existing IP leverage:** claim/source receipts, contradiction handling,
  policy provenance and unresolved-remainder preservation.
- **Minimum sellable product:** machine-readable endpoint inventory, dated
  provider-policy snapshots, workload comparison and change receipts.
- **Distribution path:** privacy-engineering communities, AI governance teams,
  security newsletters and CI/config integrations.
- **Expected Frazer dependency:** low only if policy maintenance and review are
  productized; otherwise it risks turning into recurring interpretation work.
- **Maintenance/support burden:** high; policies, endpoints and contract-specific
  exceptions change continuously and invite legal/support questions.
- **Falsification condition:** teams require counsel-backed determinations rather
  than evidence tooling, or verified policy maintenance costs exceed realistic
  subscription revenue.
- **Disposition:** `COMMERCIAL_WATCH`; Forge state remains `Φ — LIMINAL`.

All three pass the hard Frazer-dependency gate in their proposed self-serve
form. Any future sales motion that requires Frazer's recurring presence,
customer-specific reconstruction or emergency account continuity fails the
gate regardless of revenue potential.
