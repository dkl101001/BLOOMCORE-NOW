# Public/Private Boundary

## Public in this repository

- schemas and validators for weekly release metadata;
- deterministic test, license, boundary and packaging tools;
- source inspection and evidence-receipt examples;
- bounded hosted demonstration;
- documentation, limitations and public lineage.

## Excluded

- private BLOOMCORE orchestration;
- private ECA synthesis or candidate-family selection;
- proprietary coherence or trajectory scoring;
- identity-bearing substrate memory;
- hidden routing, production adapters and complete assembly paths;
- credentials, private datasets and operational user information.
